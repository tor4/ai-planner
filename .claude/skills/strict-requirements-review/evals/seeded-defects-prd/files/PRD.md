# PRD: Team Calendar Sync

Owner: Product team. Status: draft.

## Problem
Teams miss meetings because their calendars are out of sync across tools.

## Goals
- Users love the new sync.
- Sync is fast.

## Requirements

### Must
1. Read-only access to the user's Google Calendar. The app never writes to the user's calendar.
2. When two events conflict, the app moves the later event to the next free slot in the user's calendar.
3. Smart suggestions (see Should 1) are shown next to every event.
4. Support Google Calendar, Outlook, etc.

### Should
1. Smart suggestions for meeting times based on the user's history, shown as needed.

## Timeline
Release in Q3.
